Dear Areshey,

I don't really know where to begin, because some feelings are difficult to put into words.

I didn't make this little universe to impress you with beautiful colours, flowers or a few lines on a screen. I made it because sometimes I have things in my heart that I cannot explain properly when I speak.

I know you don't like compliments, so I won't fill this letter with them.

You don't always have to be happy. You don't always have to have the right words. You can have your quiet days, your confusing moments and your own way of seeing the world. You don't have to pretend to be someone else.

I cannot understand every thought in your mind, and I won't pretend that I can. But I would always like to try.

I don't know what tomorrow holds for us. I don't know what answer you have in your heart, and I don't want this letter to make you feel that you owe me one.

If your answer is yes, I'll value that sincerely. And if your answer is no, I'll still be grateful that I got the chance to make something special for you.

I hope one day, when you see flowers, you remember this little surprise and smile. Not because you had to. Just because you wanted to.

With sincerity,
Ali ❤️
