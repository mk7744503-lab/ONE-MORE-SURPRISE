A letter for Areshey

A little something from Ali.

To Areshey,

I don't really know where to begin, because some feelings are difficult to put into words.

I didn't make this little world to impress you with beautiful colours, flowers or a few lines on a screen. I made it because sometimes I have things in my heart that I cannot explain properly when I speak.

I know you don't like compliments, so I won't fill this letter with them. I just want you to know something.

You don't always have to be happy. You don't always have to have the right words. You don't have to pretend that everything is okay when it isn't.

You can have your quiet days, your confusing moments, your little moods and your own way of seeing the world. I don't expect you to be someone else just to make things easier for me.

I know I cannot understand every thought in your mind, and I won't pretend that I can. But I would always like to try.

Maybe that's what makes something meaningful: not finding someone perfect, but finding someone whose feelings you genuinely want to understand.

I don't know what tomorrow holds for us. I don't know what answer you have in your heart, and I don't want this letter to make you feel that you owe me one.

If your answer is yes, I'll value that sincerely. And if your answer is no, I'll still be grateful that I got the chance to make something special for you.

But before you close this letter, I want you to remember one thing:

You were never just a name on a screen to me. You were someone I wanted to make a little effort for, even if all I could do was create a small world of flowers and words.

I hope one day, when you see flowers, you remember this little surprise and smile.

Not because you had to. Just because you wanted to.

With sincerity,
Ali ❤️
