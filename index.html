Dear Areshey,

Aaj hum mile hain, aur shayad abhi ek doosre ko jaanne ka safar shuru hi hua hai. Main nahi chahta ke chand mulaqaton ko bade-bade daawon ya kahaniyon mein badal doon. Kuch cheezon ko waqt ke saath apni jagah banane dena zyada khoobsurat hota hai. Lekin ek chhoti si baat zaroor kehna chahta hoon. Main chahta tha ke tumhare liye kuch aisa banaun jo rozmarra ki cheezon se thoda alag ho. Isliye ye website, ye baarah flowers, har flower ka apna message, dinner ka chhota sa plan aur ye letter. Har detail perfect ho, ye zaroori nahi. Lekin har detail mein meri apni mehnat aur ek sachchi si niyyat zaroor hai. Mujhe nahi pata tum is surprise ko dekh kar kya mehsoos karogi. Main tumhari taraf se kuch assume bhi nahi karna chahta. Bas meri taraf se ye ek chhoti si koshish hai — ke tumhare chehre par ek muskurahat aa jaye, aur tumhein ek aisa moment mile jo thoda different ho. Zindagi mein har mulaqat ka koi bada matlab ho, zaroori nahi. Kabhi kabhi kisi naye insaan ke liye ek khoobsurat gesture karna bhi apne aap mein kaafi hota hai. Agar tumhein is poori chhoti si duniya mein koi ek flower, ek line ya ek detail pasand aa jaye, toh samajhna meri mehnat kamyab ho gayi. Tumhari marzi, tumhara waqt aur tumhari feelings apni jagah mukammal ahmiyat rakhti hain. Is surprise ke peeche koi pressure ya expectation nahi. Bas ek nayi mulaqat ke naam, ek achhe moment ke naam, aur ek muskurahat ke naam. Thank you for being a part of this little surprise.

With sincerity,
Ali Areeshay ♥
